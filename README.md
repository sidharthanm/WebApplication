#Learning Web Development Without AI

**AI helps you learn fast, It pushes you into the illusion of learning, faster.**

I have created this repo today to track and share my progress of learning to build apps and stress testing it without the help of AI.

Whenever I feel the necessity of having to use AI, It would be clearly mentioned where and for what I had used it.

I have been on a journey learning DSA and grinding leetcode, I want that to change into building systems and applications and stress testing it on lower spec hardware.

This idea is heavily inspired from @arjay_the_dev from insta.

I am going to start by building basic apps without even frontend and work on how DSA and design choices impact the performance.

Basically I will be putting theory into practice, I am tired of just grinding theory and DSA. I genuinely crave to build stuff.

Follow outline.md for how I am going to structure the journey/
